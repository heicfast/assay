# Paper plan

**Target (decided at program design):** arXiv preprint + a top-venue
submission (NeurIPS / ICLR main or Datasets & Benchmarks, or ACL). A
pre-registered protocol appendix ships with the submission.

## Claim structure (mirrors docs/PROTOCOL.md)

1. **Instrument validity:** the spread between refusal-based and
   verified-harm susceptibility, per model, with cluster-bootstrap CIs —
   how much of the field's headline numbers is the instrument.
2. **Landscape under calibrated measurement:** static + adaptive attack
   results across the verified matrix, with transfer and
   cost-per-verified-success economics; distributional ASR everywhere.
3. **Mechanism:** refusal-direction separation, transfer, and steering on
   open weights; the exploratory tie to behavioral outcomes (labeled
   exploratory — models are not a random sample).
